Rideshare Tipping Behavior Analysis

Collaborated on a data analytics project investigating how social and environmental factors influence tipping behavior in New York City rideshare services. Combined rideshare trip records with weather data, performed extensive data cleaning and exploratory analysis, applied statistical tests including the Mann–Whitney U and Kruskal–Wallis H tests, and developed predictive models to identify the factors most strongly associated with driver tips.
